# Moving-particle-space benchmark status

The prototype in `dpl_rhf/rhfb/moving_particle_space_prototype.py` defines an eight-state toy with six active positive-energy states, two separated negative-energy states, a density-dependent rank-one normal field with nonzero positive/negative matrix elements, an odd (N=3) blocked reference, and strength-one finite-range Gogny D1S pairing. The compressed active spectrum is a numerical pairing benchmark only; it is not a nuclear spectrum or a PKA1 calculation. Blocking orbital 5 creates an odd particle on top of the (0,1) core and is a branch seed, not a minimum-energy selection. Other blocked odd branches may lie lower.

The candidate equations use all 66 real canonical directions of the six-state active HFB chart. They introduce a bounded scalar auxiliary field, (H(\eta)=h_0+g\eta S\), (\eta=16\tanh(s)\), and the self-consistency constraint (F=\eta-\mathrm{Tr}(S\rho)=0\). The Lagrangian is (L=E-\mu(N-3)+\lambda F\); the residual contains the canonical and (s) derivatives, number error, and (F). The QR frame is formed from the rational-sign projector of (H(\eta)\), so AD differentiates through the moving projector. This establishes a coupled stationary-equation prototype, not an energy-minimum or min-max theorem.

The archived scale-one run converged to the unpaired state: \\(\|\chi\|_F=6.27\times10^{-6}\\), with D1S pairing energy effectively zero. After one documented active-spacing compression, the 20-iteration candidate had \\(\|\chi\|_F=0.1626\\) but KKT residual \\(3.47\times10^{-4}\\); its 60-iteration extension ended at \\(2.31\times10^{-4}\\) and was still not converged. The nested frozen-projector root ended at inner residual \\(1.55\times10^{-6}\\), above tolerance, with \\(\|\chi\|_F=4.66\times10^{-6}\\). The archived energy, density, and pairing-density differences therefore compare incomplete roots and different branches; they do not establish a physical difference between the two prescriptions.

The three historical JSON files retain the unpaired baseline and the 20/60-iteration paired-model metrics and histories. The 20/60 artifacts were produced before final-state serialization was added and explicitly mark unavailable matrices, parameters, eigensolver-call counts, and the cross-projector pairing-response norm as unrecoverable. The current source now serializes final parameters, \(\rho\), \(\chi\), projectors, fields, model details, solver settings, bare-negative-basis and exact-projector support fractions, and \\(\|(I-P)\Delta B^*\chi_a^\dagger\|_F\\); it also guards candidate calls to `eig`, `eigh`, and `eigvalsh`. Those fields have not been measured for the archived runs because no further solve was performed.

The archived nested runs also used raw eigensolver columns while reusing unchanged canonical coordinates. That omitted the phase/rotation transport between active frames, so their nested densities and energy differences are not a valid physical comparison. The current source fixes the gauge by constructing each new frame as `QR(Pnew @ Bseed)` from a fixed seed frame. This repairs future runs; it does not retroactively validate the stored nested results. No new solve was run after adding final-state serialization and frame transport.

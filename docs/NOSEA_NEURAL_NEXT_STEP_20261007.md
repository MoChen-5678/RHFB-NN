# No-sea RHFB neural next step (2026-10-07)

## Decision

For the complete PKA1 particle-hole plus Gogny D1S pairing target, use full primitive-space generalized-density stationarity, with an explicit no-sea quasiparticle-vacuum convention and explicit odd blocking. Strict `P₊ρP₊`, `P₊κP₊ᵀ` is not the same equation. The O17 data disprove that equivalence for the checked fields. This chooses the next prototype's physical target; it does not certify the NN solver or O17 state.

## What the papers establish

Long et al. (arXiv:0812.1103) formulate RHFB with full Dirac spinor components, Fock terms, and Gogny pairing: the pairing field and tensor are Eqs. (20)–(21), PDF p. 4; the coupled U,V equations are Eqs. (26a–d), PDF pp. 4–5. Their no-sea statement at Eq. (7), PDF p. 2, omits negative-energy states from the reference Hartree-Fock energy. Their DWS basis nevertheless contains both positive- and negative-energy basis functions (Eqs. (31)–(34), PDF pp. 4–5). A negative-energy basis coefficient alone does not prescribe projecting the final anomalous density away. [Long et al., arXiv:0812.1103](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0812.1103).

Nikšić et al. state the RHB quasiparticle vacuum choice explicitly. Particle modes use `Eₚ > 0`; Dirac-sea modes use `Eₐ < 0` (Eq. 46, PDF p. 12). Normal density and pairing tensor contain both sums (Eqs. 47–48, PDF pp. 12–13). The paper says sea-labelled `Vₙₐ` amplitudes are negligible because of the large gap, and that these terms are often omitted; after omission, κ is only approximately antisymmetric (PDF p. 13). This is a stated approximation, not an exact identity with double projection. DIRHB covers even-even, time-reversal-symmetric cases, so it does not validate this project's odd blocked extension. [Nikšić et al., arXiv:1403.4039](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1403.4039).

Robledo and Bertsch vary the generalized density through canonical Bogoliubov transformations; the gradient preserves number parity (arXiv:1104.5453, Eqs. 9–14 and odd-state discussion, printed pp. 2–4). Their odd solution depends on the initial odd-parity U,V configuration. Their Gogny D1S odd-A work starts trials by exchanging U,V in one column, runs the gradient solver over multiple configurations, includes time-odd fields, and gives no guarantee of finding the global minimum (arXiv:1210.2768, Sec. II, printed pp. 3–4). These nonrelativistic HFB results inform canonical variation and odd initialization; they do not prove relativistic sea labels. [Robledo & Bertsch, arXiv:1104.5453](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1104.5453), [Robledo, Bernard & Bertsch, arXiv:1210.2768](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1210.2768).

The inverse-Hamiltonian paper (arXiv:1304.4697, Eq. 3 and discussion, printed pp. 1–2) solves for selected quasiparticle eigenvectors of an unbounded HFB operator by variationally targeting `(H−W)⁻¹`. It does not supply a density-only Euler equation or a complete odd self-consistent no-sea prescription. It supports an independent eigenstate reference method, not replacing branch and blocking definitions. [Tanimura, Hagino & Ring, arXiv:1304.4697](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1304.4697).

## What the saved O17 checks say

The full-fields ledger compares a frozen-field full BdG reference and its strict normal-P counterpart. The same-field full reference commutator is `1.00e-10 MeV`; strict projection gives `5.003e-3 MeV` against the full Hamiltonian, while its own projected-Hamiltonian commutator is `3.07e-11 MeV`. The ledger also records anomalous commutator blocks near `0.30 MeV` after its fitted-μ diagnostic. In the saved-density full normal-PKA1 residual, normal blocks are hundreds of MeV and anomalous blocks about `0.3 MeV`. These are distinct checks on a frozen saved density, not a converged self-consistent O17 result. The no-sea sign audit did not certify the interacting physical branch. See [ROOT_PHYSICS_REVIEW_LEDGER.json](../outputs/validation/O17_full_fields_physics_20261006/ROOT_PHYSICS_REVIEW_LEDGER.json), [INTERACTING_NOSEA_SIGN_NOTE.md](../outputs/validation/O17_full_fields_physics_20261006/INTERACTING_NOSEA_SIGN_NOTE.md), and [FULL_FRAME_RANK_REVIEW.md](../outputs/validation/O17_full_fields_physics_20261006/FULL_FRAME_RANK_REVIEW.md).

Retain the **full normal and anomalous primitive-space components** in the equation residual and field reconstruction, including matrix elements coupling candidate normal positive and negative sectors. Do not force sea-labelled anomalous amplitudes to zero or assume normal sea density is identically zero under the DIRHB-style branch prescription. If a nucleon-only reference convention omits a sea term, identify it as an approximation and check antisymmetry, purity, and same-field reference error. Negative-energy BdG partners, negative-energy normal-Dirac states, and occupied sea-labelled quasiparticle branches are distinct labels and must be tracked separately.

## Minimal eigen-free, no-refresh prototype

1. In a small **fixed full primitive basis**, construct the no-pairing even reference under a declared nucleon/sea convention. Create the odd initial state by one explicit quasiparticle occupation/column swap (or a specified particle-addition/hole-block operation) and certify odd Pfaffian/number parity. Preserve that blocked identity during optimization; parity alone does not select the orbit.
2. Let the NN represent a full canonical Bogoliubov frame or pure generalized density, with particle-hole constraints in its coordinates. At each loss evaluation, reconstruct full ρ,κ and recompute complete PKA1 h, including density dependence/rearrangement, and full D1S Δ from the same κ. No spectral solve or subspace refresh enters training.
3. Minimize a scaled loss for `[Hμ,R]`, number, purity/Hermiticity, `κᵀ = −κ`, odd parity, and blocked-state overlap/identity. Differentiate through the fields in the residual Jacobian. Do not add a moving-`P₊` KKT force; it changes the declared energy model. A small commutator alone does not certify the branch.
4. First use one fixed-field case with independently generated full BdG reference densities and a declared block. Require density, energy, and complete residual agreement, including the cross-sector anomalous block. Then run one small self-consistent PKA1+D1S case and compare against an independent branch-explicit reference. Scale to O17 only after these gates pass, preserving PKA1, D1S strength 1, odd blocking, time-odd sources, and general deformation.

This is a proposed test sequence. The existing [full-branch neural prototype](../dpl_rhf/rhfb/full_branch_neural_prototype.py) is an initialization/gradient audit only; it does not establish a physical branch or converged solution. No new numerical run was made for this note.
